Practical Tips

🏠 Accommodation

St. Pauli or Altona. Large apt on Airbnb for 10+ guests. €400–800/night total. Book 6–9 months ahead.

🚆 Train Option

EuroCity Express is a great group experience. City-to-city, no airport hassle. Depart ~07:22, arrive ~12:00.

🎟️ HSV Tickets

Buy the moment 2026–27 fixtures drop at hsv.de. Bundesliga games sell out quickly.

🍺 Local Beer

Astra is Hamburg's local brew — order it everywhere near the stadium and Reeperbahn.

🚲 Beer Bike

Book 4–6 weeks ahead minimum. Very popular with groups, can sell out on peak weekends.

📍 Location

St. Pauli puts you 5 min walk from the Reeperbahn, harbour, and great restaurants.
