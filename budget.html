For a group of 10 — the total difference

Budapest saves roughly €950–1,300 in total group spend compared to Hamburg, and €1,400–2,250 compared to Paris. That's real money that stays in your pockets for more drinks, better dinners, or an extra activity.

Paris is the most expensive but delivers the most iconic experience — PSG at the Parc des Princes, the Seine by night, and the world's greatest city as your backdrop. If the group can stretch the budget, it's worth every euro.
