For a group of 10 — the total difference

Budapest saves roughly 3.000–6.500 kr in total group spend compared to Hamburg, and 7.000–14.500 kr compared to Paris. That's real money that stays in your pockets for more drinks, better dinners, or an extra activity.

Note that the gap to Hamburg is narrower than it used to look: the price-checked Budapest flight came in above the old desk estimate, while the Hamburg and Paris flight rows have not been re-checked. If their real fares also land higher, Budapest's lead widens again.

Paris is the most expensive but delivers the most iconic experience — PSG at the Parc des Princes, the Seine by night, and the world's greatest city as your backdrop. If the group can stretch the budget, it's worth every krone.
