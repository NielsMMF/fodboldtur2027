Verdicts

🇭🇺 Budapest — Best overall value

One of Europe's most established group-trip destinations. Significantly cheaper across every category, and the activities scene is purpose-built for lads' weekends. The ruin bars are unlike anything in Northern Europe. Your money goes roughly twice as far here compared to Paris.

🇩🇪 Hamburg — Best football experience

If Bundesliga quality is non-negotiable, Hamburg delivers. The Volksparkstadion at 57,000 is a proper top-level atmosphere. The train from Copenhagen is a relaxed, airport-free group journey. The Reeperbahn and Beer Bike are uniquely Hamburg.

🇫🇷 Paris — Most iconic, highest stakes

PSG at the Parc des Princes is a genuine bucket-list football experience. Paris is pricier, but the city delivers on every front — nightlife in Pigalle, the Seine by night, world-class food. Worth it if budget is less of a concern.
