Shooting Range — Two Options, Pick On The Day

A. Churchill Shooting Range

~710–1.010 kr/person
Holló utca 6, District VII · Open daily 10:00–18:00 · +36 1 785 3334 · ~1 hr, 8 firearms
ProsClosest to the apartment — a few minutes' walk from Szimpla Kert, so no transport at all. Ex-military instructors. Best weapon lineup for the money: 1898 antiques and WWII classics through to modern sniper rifles.
ConsMost expensive of the two, and the session is short at ~1 hour. Range sits on the -1 floor of a parking garage — functional rather than atmospheric. Closes at 18:00, so a 13:00 start is the latest that works comfortably.

B. Hardcore / Capital Shooting Range

~375–675 kr/person
Dob utca 89, District VII · M2 to Blaha Lujza tér or tram 4/6 · Max group size 12 · 4.8★ (97 reviews)
ProsClear value winner — the "Taste-Off-Everything" package is ~20,000 HUF (~375 kr) and covers 9mm through AK-47 and shotguns. Group cap of 12 fits us exactly. Purpose-built indoor facility, strongly reviewed.
ConsRoughly a 15-min walk east from the ruin-bar core rather than around the corner. Less detail published on session length and instructors — worth asking when booking.
ℹ️ Both venues sit at District VII addresses and are marketed under overlapping brands — Hardcore and Capital both list Dob utca 89, so they are very likely the same physical range sold through two booking sites. Get a quote from both pages and take the better one. Prices are indicative; confirm a firm group rate for 7–12 when booking.
Bars & Nightlife — The Shortlist

Szimpla Kert — the icon

Free entry
Kazinczy u. 14, District VII · Fri & Sat 12:00–04:00 · Opened 2002, the original ruin bar
Why goNon-negotiable first stop — a bombed-out tenement filled with junk sculpture and a dozen bars. Free entry, opens early enough for a Friday afternoon start.
Watch outPeak tourist trap after ~21:00 and rammed at weekends. Hit it early, then move on.

Élesztőház — the beer stop

Beer ~15–20 kr
Tűzoltó u. 22, District IX · Thu–Sat 15:00–03:00 · ~30 rotating taps in a former glassworks
Why goBest beer selection in the city — Horizont, Fehér Nyúl, MONYO plus a cask ale bar and a wine bar for the non-beer drinkers. Sprawling courtyard swallows a group of 12 without a booking.
Watch outNot in the Jewish Quarter — it's in District IX, ~25 min walk or a short tram from Szimpla. Better used as a Saturday pre-match stop, since it's the same district as Groupama Arena, than squeezed into the Friday crawl.

Mazel Tov — the civilised one

Mains ~90–135 kr
Akácfa u. 47, District VII · Sun–Thu 12:00–23:00, Fri–Sat 12:00–24:00 · +36 70 626 4280
Why goGenuinely good food — shakshuka, hummus, lamb kofta — in a garden-like covered courtyard with live music. The strongest candidate for the Friday group dinner, and it's 30 seconds from Instant-Fogas for the night after.
Watch outCloses at midnight, so it's a dinner venue not a late-night one. Reserve for 7–12 in advance: reservation@mazeltov.hu.

Instant-Fogas Complex — the main event

Free before 22:00, then 40–75 kr
Akácfa u. 51, District VII · Daily 18:00–06:00 · Seven venues in one building
Why goBudapest's biggest party complex — Instant, Fogas, Unterwelt, Liebling, LÄRM, Frame and Robot under one roof, so a group that wants different music can split up and still be in the same place. LÄRM has a Funktion-One rig and is a serious techno room.
Watch outLÄRM is a room inside this complex, not a separate venue — no need to plan a trip to both. Arrive before 22:00 to dodge the cover charge.

Akvárium Klub — the alternative

Varies by event
Erzsébet tér 12, District V · Fri–Sat 17:00–06:00 · Under the square, live venue + club
Why goThe city's main indoor concert venue doubling as a late club. Worth checking the listings — if there's a gig on the Friday or Saturday it beats another ruin bar.
Watch outClosed Sunday through Wednesday, so it only works on our Fri/Sat nights. Ticketed events sell out — book online in advance. It's a ~15 min walk out of District VII.
⚠️ Saturday cruise — read before booking. The well-known Budapest "boat party" cruises (Ultimate Boat Parties, Danube Beat) are evening departures at 465–555 kr/person with open bar — they clash directly with an 18:00 kick-off and cost roughly double the charter option. For the 13:00 slot, book a private charter instead (Private Cruise Budapest, nightcruise.hu): from ~3.375 kr for the boat, daytime departures from 12:00, drinks usually charged separately or brought aboard.

🍺 Friday 16 April — everything below is inside District VII, walking distance from the apartment

Hopaholic Beer

Beer ~20–30 kr
Akácfa u. 38, District VII · Thu–Sat 16:00–02:00 · 10 taps + 250–300 bottles and cans
Why goThe right answer for people who want different beers rather than lots of one. Genuine beer-geek selection — world imports alongside Hungarian breweries — and it's 200 m from Szimpla, so nobody has to leave the crawl to get it.
Watch outSmall room, no table service for a group of 12. Fine standing, but if we want seats, go early. Alternative: Léhűtő, Holló u. 12–14 — 6 Hungarian taps, quieter, next door to the Churchill range.

Bar Pharma Cocktails

Cocktails ~75–105 kr
Kazinczy u. 35, District VII · Fri–Sat 18:00–03:00 · Apothecary-themed speakeasy
Why goProper mixology with an exclusive feel — drinks served in medicine bottles and lab glass, low light, no ruin-bar chaos. Crucially it's on the same street as Szimpla, so switching to this from the ruin bars is a two-minute walk, not a change of plan.
Watch outIntimate room — don't roll in 12-strong unannounced. Split into two groups or call ahead. Alternative: Black Swan, Klauzál u. 32, Fri–Sat 16:00–02:00 — same speakeasy idea, opens earlier.

Füge Udvar Bodega + Games

Billiards ~30 kr/hr
Klauzál u. 19–21, District VII · Daily 16:00–04:00 · 1,200 m² courtyard complex
Why goExactly the brief — darts, ping pong (bats and balls provided), billiards, air hockey and foosball, in a scruffy ruin-bar courtyard rather than a sports-bar box. Big enough to absorb 12 people without booking a thing, and it runs to 04:00.
Watch outGames can be reserved Wed–Fri 16:00–21:00 only — outside that window it's first-come. On a Friday night the tables go fast, so claim one on arrival.

SCORE! Sport Bar & Billiard Sport Games

Beer ~15–20 kr
Dob u. 65, District VII · Fri–Sat 16:00–02:00 · 400 m² cellar, 15+ screens, billiard tables · +36 70 733 6222
Why goWhatever is on Friday night, it's on here — 15+ screens angled at long shared benches, which suits a group of 12 far better than a room of small tables. It also has billiard tables, so it covers both the screens and the pool option in one venue, and it's the latest-closing sports bar in District VII.
Watch outDoesn't open until 16:00, and it's closed Mondays. Windowless cellar — fine at night, grim in daylight. Book a table at reservations.scorebudapest.hu for a big fixture.

High Note SkyBar Cocktails

Hercegprímás u. 5, District V (Aria Hotel roof) · Smart casual · Booking advised

Noiret Billiards & Darts Saloon Bodega + Games Cocktails

Table hire ~30–45 kr/hr
Dessewffy u. 8–10, District VI · Daily 10:00–04:00 · 10 Brunswick tables, 5 steel + 8 electronic dartboards
Why goThe most flexible venue on this page — a real cocktail bar and ten full-size pool tables and thirteen dartboards under one roof, open until 04:00. Covers two of the four options at once, so it's the safest choice if the group can't settle on one.
Watch outPool tables can be booked online, but seating tables only by phone (+36 1 331 6103) — call ahead for 7–12 on a Saturday. Old-school saloon rather than a scruffy bodega, if that matters on the night.

Champs Sport Pub Sport

Beer ~15–20 kr
Dohány u. 20, District VII · Tue–Sun 12:00–23:30, closed Mon · 200+ seats, 40 screens, 3 projectors
Why goThe city's flagship sports pub and the obvious pre-match build-up spot — 40 screens, three giant projectors and 1,900+ sports channels, so the earlier kick-offs are all on. With 200+ seats a group of 12 walks in without booking, and it's a 5-minute walk from the apartment before heading to the stadium.

Practical Tips

🏠 Accommodation

District VI/VII. See shortlisted options above. 2.250–3.750 kr/night total. Book 6–9 months ahead.

💶 Currency

Hungarian Forint (HUF). Cards widely accepted; carry some cash for bars.

🚕 Transport

Bolt app (like Uber) — excellent and very cheap. Metro also reliable. Rides: 20–40 kr.

📱 Book Ahead

Boat cruise needs advance booking. Shooting range: pick the venue on the day, but ring ahead to hold a slot for 7–12.

💰 Value

Beer ~10 kr, dinner 75–110 kr/person, taxis 20–40 kr. One of Europe's best-value cities.

⚽ Tickets

Check ftc.hu summer 2026. Confirm home game before booking flights.
