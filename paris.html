Friday · 16 April

Arrival — Montmartre & Pigalle

07:00
–
10:00
✈️ Fly CPH → CDG (Paris Charles de Gaulle)
~2h 15min direct. Aim for a 07:00–08:00 departure. Budget 750–1.500 kr per person return — desk estimate, not yet price-checked. From CDG take the RER B direct to central Paris (~45 min, 90 kr). Or taxi ~450 kr flat rate to the city.
~11:30
🏠 Check In — Group Apartment, Montmartre or Le Marais
Large 4–5 bedroom apartment in Montmartre (18th) for the Pigalle nightlife strip on the doorstep, or Le Marais (3rd/4th) for a more central base. Search Airbnb for "Paris apartment 10 guests". Drop bags, grab a café.
13:30
🏎️ Go-Karting — Racing Kart de Paris Active
Paris has excellent indoor karting facilities with professional tracks and timed grand prix sessions. Great competitive opener for the group. ~260–375 kr/person. Book ahead for groups.
17:00
🗼 Aperitivo — Sacré-Cœur Steps
Grab wine and beer from a nearby shop and head up to the steps below Sacré-Cœur in Montmartre for sunset views over Paris. A Parisian institution — groups do this constantly. Free and unforgettable.
19:30
🍷 Group Dinner — Montmartre Bistro
Book a long table at a classic Parisian bistro in Montmartre. Steak frites, duck confit, carafe wine. Paris restaurants are pricier but the quality is exceptional. ~260–410 kr/person with wine.
21:30+
🎭 Pigalle Night Out Night
Pigalle (aka "Pig-Alle") is Paris's legendary nightlife district — bars, clubs, and the Moulin Rouge all on one stretch. Start with cocktails at a bar on Rue de Douai, then into La Cigale or Le Divan du Monde. Lively until 5am.
02
Saturday · 17 April

Seine Cruise + PSG Match Night

10:00
☕ Late Breakfast at the Apartment
Grab fresh croissants and pain au chocolat from the local boulangerie — there will be one within 100 metres. Strong café au lait. Recover and plan.
13:00
🛥️ Seine Party Cruise Active
Private or semi-private group boat cruise on the Seine — passing the Eiffel Tower, Notre-Dame, and Musée d'Orsay from the water. Options with an open bar and DJ available. ~300–490 kr/person. Book ahead for groups of 7–12.
15:30
🍺 Pre-Match Drinks — Parc des Princes Area
The Parc des Princes is in the 16th arrondissement. The bars around Exelmans and Michel-Ange–Molitor metro stations fill with PSG supporters before home games. Join the pre-match atmosphere.
~18:00
kick-off
⚽ PSG Home Match — Parc des Princes Match
~48,000 capacity in one of Europe's most atmospheric club stadiums. PSG's Ligue 1 fixtures in April are typically high-stakes title-race games. Tickets 225–600 kr — book immediately at psg.fr when fixtures drop (summer 2026). Metro line 9 to Exelmans or line 10 to Michel-Ange–Molitor.
21:00+
🌃 Post-Match — Oberkampf or Le Marais Night
Post-match beers near the stadium, then head east to Oberkampf (Paris's best bar-hopping street, unpretentious, great for groups) or Le Marais for a mix of cocktail bars and clubs. Both go until 5–6am.
03
Sunday · 18 April

Canal Saint-Martin + Departure

10:30
☕ Recovery Brunch — Canal Saint-Martin
The Canal Saint-Martin neighbourhood (10th arr.) is Paris's coolest Sunday brunch spot. Lined with cafés, brunch spots, and locals reading the paper by the water. ~110–190 kr/person.
13:00
🚶 Canal Walk + Final Drinks
Walk along the canal, pick up a few final beers from a shop and enjoy them by the waterside — perfectly Parisian. Then back for bags.
16:00
✈️ Head to Airport — Fly Home CDG → CPH
RER B from central Paris to CDG takes ~45 min. Allow plenty of time — CDG is a large airport. Multiple evening flights back to Copenhagen (Air France, SAS, Norwegian, easyJet).
Practical Tips

🏠 Accommodation

Montmartre for Pigalle nightlife access; Le Marais for central location. 4–5 bed on Airbnb. 3.750–6.750 kr/night total. Book early — Paris fills fast.

🚇 Transport

Metro is excellent and cheap (~15 kr/ride). Get a carnet of 10 tickets. Avoid taxis at rush hour — metro is faster.

⚽ PSG Tickets

Buy immediately at psg.fr when fixtures drop (summer 2026). High-demand games sell out in hours.

💶 Budget

Paris is pricier than Budapest or Hamburg. Budget 110–150 kr for dinner mains, 60–90 kr for cocktails. Worth it.

✈️ Airport

CDG is large — arrive 2.5 hrs before departure. RER B to CDG: ~90 kr, ~45 min from central Paris.

🥐 Boulangerie

Find your nearest bakery on Day 1. Fresh croissants each morning — non-negotiable in Paris.
